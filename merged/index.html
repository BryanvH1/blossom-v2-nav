Rules

  • One page, one name, one place. Every current page lands in exactly one area. URLs don't change.
  • A role sees only the areas it can do something in. The permission level decides it, so a page is never shown to someone who can't open it.
  • The home is called Today, and everyone lands there. Same zones, same places. Only the hero and the details change by role, season and situation, and a situation (a frost night, a failed pump) outranks the season.
  • Logging is a button, not a destination. The + opens the things this role records. Menus are for looking things up.
  • Never show 0, N/A or an empty chart. Hide it or say the next-best true thing. At most one setup suggestion per screen.
  • Needs you only holds what this person can approve. It's driven by the approval rules below.

Still to decide with John

  1. Top bar or sidebar on desktop. A sidebar that expands the current area's pages inline would cut the change for today's users the most. The top bar gives more width to maps and tables.
  2. Map for run-the-farm roles. It's merged as a chip inside Beds so + can sit in the centre. The alternative is Today · Beds · + · Map · More, which puts Apply in More.
  3. Rainfall. The Sep 21 proposal put it in Weather; John keeps it in Water at /water/rainfall. It's merged under Water to keep the routes.
  4. Timing. Ship between January and March, not during spray season or harvest. Rework the Help Center once the labels are final.

Who approves work orders (fills Needs you)

Crop Manager approves spray, nutrient, water and vine orders. Equipment & Building Manager approves equipment orders. Operations Manager and Owner approve anything. Operator approves its own, as today. Nobody else does, including Admin. An approval lands in that person's Needs you on Today, with the one button that clears it.

How the seats map to the built-in roles

  • Owner is Organization Admin once BLO-842 makes it a true superset of Farm admin.
  • Operations Manager is Farm admin.
  • Crop Manager is Operator with harvest yields added and equipment trimmed to request-only.
  • Equipment & Building Manager is Equipment Foreman with delete, Reports view and Calendar edit.
  • Finance Manager and Admin are new seats; John's Accountant and Office Manager tab bars fit them.
  • Application Foreman, Equipment Foreman, Applicator and Mechanic stay as documented.
