The same seven areas, on a computer

The merged navigation at desktop width. The farm switcher sits on the left, each area opens a menu of its pages, and + Log is on every screen. Pick a role, open the menus, and flip the layout to compare John's top bar with a sidebar, which is the open question. Phone version · Original nine-area proposal

What changes from today's sidebar

  • 19 sidebar rows in three groups become 7 areas, each with a short menu.
  • The sidebar's farm card and season tile become one switcher.
  • + Log is always in the bar, so recording something never means hunting for a page.
  • Routes don't change. Watch the address bar as you click through.

Top bar or sidebar

Top bar (John): more width for maps and tables, and it matches the phone's area-first model. Sidebar: closer to what users have today, and the pages stay visible without opening a menu. Both use the same areas, names and routes, so the choice doesn't affect anything else.

Rules carried over

  • An area with one page for a role is a plain link, not a menu.
  • A role sees only the areas it can do something in.
  • Clicking the logo always goes to Today.
