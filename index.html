Rules that make it work

  • One level of nesting. Every top-level area is a page with a tab strip. Nothing hides below a tab. On desktop the sidebar shows the ten areas and expands the active one's tabs inline.
  • Labels that fit one line on a phone. "Crop", "Equipment", "Data" instead of the long forms. The long form lives in the caption under the label.
  • Two dashboards is one too many. The landing page becomes Home. Charts, reports and PUR transfer become Data. Nobody has to guess which dashboard they want.
  • Reference dissolves into the places it describes. Varieties sit with Beds, Products with Applications, Pests with Scouting, Users under Settings.
  • Logging is a button, not a destination. The green plus opens a sheet of the six things a grower records in the field. The menu is for looking things up.
  • Order follows the day. Orient first (Home, Map, Calendar, Weather), then the work areas, then Data at the bottom for the office.

Decided 21 Sep 2026

  1. Irrigation stays under Water. Water owns every water event. A chemigation run is also an application, so it appears under Crop › Applications with method "sprinkler" and is logged once.
  2. ICM is called Crop. Scouting, Applications, Tests, plus the Pests and Products lists.
  3. Map is a tab of Beds. That brings the menu to nine. Crop takes the freed slot in the bottom bar.

Who approves work orders

Crop Manager approves spray, nutrient, water and vine orders. Equipment & Building Manager approves equipment orders. Operations Manager and Owner approve anything. Operator approves its own, as today. Nobody else does, including Admin.

How the seats map to the built-in roles

  • Owner is Organization Admin once BLO-842 makes it a true superset of Farm admin.
  • Operations Manager is Farm admin. Nothing new to build.
  • Crop Manager is Operator with harvest yields added and equipment trimmed to request-only. The landing spot for most Operator accounts.
  • Equipment & Building Manager is Equipment Foreman with delete, Data view and Calendar edit. Buildings do not exist in the app yet.
  • Finance Manager is new: read across the farm, full control of Data.
  • Admin is new: enters and corrects records everywhere, never approves, deletes or touches settings.
  • Application Foreman, Equipment Foreman, Applicator and Mechanic stay as documented. The Application Foreman gains Calendar and Weather.
